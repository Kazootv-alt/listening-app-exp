# カフェで注文する
Maya walked into a small cafe near her office and looked at the menu on the wall.
The barista smiled and asked, "Hi there. What can I get for you today?"
"Could I have a medium latte, please?" Maya said.
"Sure. Would you like it hot or iced?"
"Hot, please. And could I get it with oat milk?"
"No problem. Anything else?" Maya looked at the pastries and pointed to a blueberry muffin. "I'll take one of those, too."
"Great. For here or to go?"
"To go, please." The barista told her the total was $6.75, and Maya paid with her phone.
A few minutes later, the barista called her name. Maya took her coffee, said thank you, and walked back to work, feeling proud that the whole conversation had been in English.
